While most of mainland Japan experiences gray, humid rainy season (Tsuyu) in June, **Hokkaido remains completely free of the rainy season**. With low humidity, blue skies, and comfortable daytime temperatures around 20–22°C (68–72°F), early June is **one of Hokkaido's best-kept seasonal travel secrets**.

Here is your comprehensive early-summer itinerary, featuring both a **step-free JR Limited Express & bus route (zero rental car needed)** and a **breathtaking scenic road trip guide**.

## Why Early June is Hokkaido's Golden Window

1. **Zero Rainy Season Humidity**:
   The seasonal rain front never reaches Hokkaido. You will enjoy crisp, sunny breezes with dry 40–50% humidity—ideal for outdoor exploring.
2. **Shakotan Peninsula Sea Urchin (Uni) Season Officially Opens**:
   Early June marks the annual opening of raw sea urchin fishing along the pristine "Shakotan Blue" coastline. Tasting freshly cracked, additive-free sweet Uni over warm rice is unforgettable.
3. **Beat the July Summer Rush**:
   Avoid the massive peak crowds and expensive hotel surcharges of mid-July and August while enjoying spacious flower parks and easier seat reservations.

<h2 id="section-drive">🚗【Scenic Road Trip】Shakotan Blue Coast & Roller Coaster Roads</h2>

Renting a vehicle gives you freedom to link dramatic ocean cliffs with rolling countryside ribbons:

- **Cape Kamui (Shakotan Peninsula)**:
  Walk the 20-minute coastal ridge trail of "Charenka's Path" leading to 300-degree panoramic ocean views of breathtaking "Shakotan Blue" waters.
- **Biei Roller Coaster Road**:
  A straight 2.5-kilometer ribbon of highway featuring dramatic vertical undulations with panoramic views of the Tokachi mountain range.
- **Wildlife Awareness**:
  Keep an eye out for wildlife, particularly Ezo deer and red foxes, when driving along mountain passes near dusk.

<h2 id="section-family">👶 Family & Stroller-Friendly Tips</h2>

- **Farm Tomita**: Smooth wooden boardwalks and paved sidewalks allow effortless stroller navigation throughout the blooming flower fields.
- **Asahiyama Zoo**: While situated on a gentle hillside, paved accessible pathways and free stroller rentals at the ticket entrances ensure a smooth visit.
- **JR Hokkaido Express Trains**: Trains offer spacious luggage storage areas and accessible family restrooms with diaper-changing tables.

---

<h2 id="section-climate">🌤️ Early June Climate & Packing Advice</h2>

- **Temperature Swings**: Afternoon highs reach a comfortable 20–22°C (68–72°F), but nights and rural hilltops drop quickly to **10–12°C (50–54°F)**.
- **What to Wear**: Pack breathable short-sleeve shirts for the day and a windbreaker, light jacket, or cardigan for breezy evenings. Don't forget sunscreen and sunglasses for bright days.
