Stretching approximately 300 meters from Aoyama-dori to the Meiji Memorial Picture Gallery, this avenue of 146 ginkgo trees transforms into an awe-inspiring golden cathedral each autumn. It is widely considered Tokyo's most iconic fall spectacle.

## 2026 Peak Season Forecast

The trees typically begin changing color in mid-November, reaching full golden saturation by late November through early December:

- **Color Transition**: Mid-November
- **Peak Golden Tunnel**: Around November 20 to December 5
- **Golden Carpet (Fallen Leaves)**: Early to mid-December

The fallen leaves creating a natural yellow carpet in early December offer an equally spectacular view.

## Pro Tip: Visit Early Morning (7:00 AM – 8:30 AM)

Between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM, the avenue becomes packed with locals and international travelers. For clear photos and tranquil morning strolls, arrive between **7:00 AM and 8:30 AM**. The gentle morning sunlight penetrating the canopy produces stunning reflections.

## Access & Nearby Stations

- **Aoyama-itchome Station** (Tokyo Metro Ginza / Hanzomon Lines, Toei Oedo Line) - Exit 1, approx. 5 min walk.
- **Kokuritsu-Kyogijo Station** (Toei Oedo Line) - Approx. 10 min walk.
- **SShinano-machi Station** (JR Chuo-Sobu Line) - Approx. 10 min walk.
