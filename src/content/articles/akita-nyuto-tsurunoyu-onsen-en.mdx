Tucked deep in the ancient beech forests of Towada-Hachimantai National Park in Akita Prefecture, **Tsurunoyu Onsen is the oldest and most legendary secluded hot spring inn (hitō) in Nyuto Onsen Village**, dating back to 1638 during the early Edo Period.

Once a private healing retreat for the feudal lords of the Satake Clan, Tsurunoyu retains its authentic thatched-roof *honjin* (samurai guard quarters) and lantern-lit stone pathways.

In winter, the entire valley transforms into an ethereal wonderland of thick white snow, where **steaming milky-white open-air baths contrast dramatically against frosted mountains**.

Here is your essential international traveler's guide to day-use bathing, etiquette, car-free transit, and reserving an unforgettable stay.

> [!WARNING]
> **Monday Outdoor Bath Cleaning Alert**:
> Every Monday, the large outdoor open-air bath is completely drained for high-pressure cleaning. **Day-use visitors can only access the indoor baths on Mondays**. To experience the iconic snowy open-air bath, schedule your visit for Tuesday through Sunday!

### The 4 Distinct Springs of Tsurunoyu
1. **Shiroyu ("White Water" / Beauty Spring)**:
   - A soothing sulfur-rich milky-white bath renowned for softening the skin and warming the body from within.
2. **Kuroyu ("Black Water" / Longevity Spring)**:
   - Slightly dark, gentler mineral water ideal for relieving joint aches and chronic fatigue.
3. **Nakanoyu ("Middle Water" / Eye Spring)**:
   - Mineral water traditionally believed to soothe tired eyes and skin irritations.
4. **Takinoyu ("Waterfall Spring" / Cascading Bath)**:
   - A forceful vertical stream targeting shoulder stiffness and muscular tension.

Crucially, the famous giant open-air bath is **directly fed from thermal springs percolating up through the pebbles lining the riverbed floor (ashi-moto yushutsu)**. You are soaking in pure, un-oxidized living mineral water straight from the earth.

## Day-Use Bathing Etiquette & Mixed-Gender Tips

### Comfort and Privacy for Women in the Mixed Bath
While the giant outdoor bath is mixed-gender (*konyoku*), Tsurunoyu is designed with thoughtful privacy measures:
- **Opaque Milky-White Water**: The mineral water is deeply opaque; once you submerge up to your shoulders, your body is completely concealed.
- **Sheltered Entry Channel**: A wooden privacy fence extends from the women's changing area directly down into the water, allowing bathers to enter in complete privacy before wading into the main pool.
- **Dedicated Women-Only Large Outdoor Bath**: For bathers who prefer complete privacy, a spacious women-only open-air bath of identical quality is located right next to the mixed pool.

### Best Time Slots to Avoid Crowds
- **Peak Quiet Window 1: Morning Opening (10:00 AM – 11:15 AM)**
  - Synchronizes with the first morning bus from Tazawako Station. The air is crystal clear and bathers are few.
- **Peak Quiet Window 2: Early Afternoon (2:00 PM – 2:30 PM)**
  - Lunch crowds begin to depart before the 3:00 PM overnight check-in rush.

## 100% Car-Free Transit Guide from Tokyo

You do not need to drive on dangerous winter ice to reach Tsurunoyu. The journey is seamlessly connected by bullet train and regional transit:

```
[Tokyo Station]
  ↓ (Akita Shinkansen "Komachi" bullet train / ~2 hr 55 min)
[Tazawako Station]
  ↓ (Ugo Kotsu "Nyuto Line" local bus / ~35 min, ¥680)
[Arupa Komakusa Bus Stop]
  ↓ (Tsurunoyu Onsen Free Shuttle Van / ~15 min)
[Tsurunoyu Onsen] Arrived!
```

> [!TIP]
> **Connecting at Arupa Komakusa**:
> - Day-use visitors can ride the complimentary Tsurunoyu shuttle van connecting directly from the **Arupa Komakusa** bus stop.
> - Call Tsurunoyu (+81-187-46-2139) from Tazawako Station or board the scheduled shuttle waiting during peak daytime hours.

## Overnight Booking Strategy (Thatched-Roof Honjin)

While day-use bathing is deeply satisfying, **staying overnight allows you to experience the kerosene lamp-lit silence, private starlit night baths, and multi-course hearth dinners**.

### Room Categories
- **Honjin (Historic Samurai Quarters)**:
  - 17th-century thatched longhouse featuring private charcoal hearths (*irori*) and kerosene lamp lighting. Highly sought after worldwide.
- **Shin-Honjin & Higashi-Honjin**:
  - Modernized traditional timber rooms equipped with heating and comfortable amenities.
- **No. 2 & No. 3 Wings**:
  - Budget-friendly, authentic tatami rooms honoring Tsurunoyu's hot-spring healing heritage.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Booking Timeline**:
> Rooms in the historic Honjin often book out six months in advance. Winter reservations open six months ahead on the 1st of each month.
