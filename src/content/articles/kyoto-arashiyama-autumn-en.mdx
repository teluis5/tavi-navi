With the forested mountain slopes painted in fiery reds and oranges behind Togetsukyo Bridge, and sunlight filtering through emerald bamboo shoots, Arashiyama is a must-visit destination in Kyoto every autumn.

## 2026 Foliage Timing & Forecast

- **Early Colors**: Early to mid-November
- **Peak Viewing**: Mid to late November
- **Late Autumn / Fallen Leaves**: Early December

The reflecting pond in Tenryu-ji Temple's Sogenchi Garden offers one of Japan's most celebrated autumn views.

## The Serene Morning Route (3 Hours)

1. **07:30 AM Togetsukyo Bridge**: Morning mist rising off the Katsura River
2. **08:15 AM Bamboo Grove**: Experience the whispering stalks with very few people around
3. **09:00 AM Tenryu-ji Temple**: Enter as gates open to enjoy the quiet reflection pond
4. **10:30 AM Main Street**: Taste freshly grilled dango and warm matcha sweets

## How to Get There

- **JR Sagano Line**: Take the rapid train from Kyoto Station to Saga-Arashiyama Station (approx. 12 minutes).
- **Hankyu Arashiyama Line**: Arashiyama Station (ideal for travelers arriving from Osaka).
- **Keifuku Electric Railroad (Randen)**: Arashiyama Station.
