Located just 45 to 50 minutes from Tokyo Station aboard the Tokaido Shinkansen, eastern Shizuoka Prefecture (Mishima and Atami) offers **the premier 2-day escape from Tokyo**. You get the best of Japan in 48 hours: pristine snow-capped Mount Fuji panoramas, oceanfront hot spring onsen, and delicious coastal seafood.

Here is your complete weekend blueprint, featuring both a **step-free bullet train itinerary (no rental car needed)** and an **expressway driving route** designed to dodge peak traffic.

1. **Unbeatable Transit Speed (45 mins to Atami / 50 mins to Mishima)**:
   You arrive before you can even finish a coffee. Spending under an hour on transit means virtually your entire weekend is spent sightseeing and unwinding.
2. **Clear Winter Skies Mean Peak Mount Fuji Visibility**:

   Warm your body in historic seaside thermal baths and feast on steamed local Kinmedai (golden-eye snapper) and fresh Suruga Bay seafood.

## 🚅【Bullet Train & No Car】2-Day Classic Itinerary from Tokyo

No international driving permit or car rental is needed. The Tokaido Shinkansen combined with direct local buses provides smooth, elevator-equipped transit.


## 🚗【Road Trip Driving Tips】Avoiding Weekend Highway Congestion

If you drive a personal or rental car, **depart Tokyo before 7:30 AM**:

- **Outbound Timing**: The Tomei Expressway near Ebina Service Area commonly clogs between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM on weekends. Leaving before 7:30 AM guarantees smooth cruising.
- **Scenic Route**: Connect seamlessly from the Shin-Tomei Expressway via the Izu-Jukan Toll-Free Expressway straight to Mishima Skywalk and Jukkokutoge Pass for 360-degree vistas.
- **Inbound Return Timing**: Sunset Sunday traffic on the inbound Tomei spikes after 4:00 PM near Yamato Tunnel. Either head back from Atami by 2:30 PM or enjoy an early dinner in Odawara/Gotemba and pass through after 7:30 PM.

## 👶 Family & Stroller-Friendly Peace of Mind

- **Mishima Skywalk Stroller Service**: For safety reasons, personal lightweight strollers are not permitted on the suspended suspension deck. However, **free off-road wide-wheel strollers are provided at the ticket gate**, making the crossing completely step-free and safe for toddlers.
- **Optimal Shinkansen Cars**: On the Tokaido Shinkansen "Kodama", booking seats near Cars 11 and 12 gives immediate access to spacious multipurpose rooms and clean accessible restrooms with diaper tables.
- **Atami Station**: The "Lusca Atami" station shopping center features clean, comfortable nursing rooms and baby changing stations.

---

## 🌤️ Weather & Attire in Mid-November & December

- **Elevation Temperature Gap**: Coastal Atami stays mild at 16–18°C (61–64°F) during the day, but high altitudes like Mishima Skywalk (250m elevation) and mountain passes experience cold crosswinds that feel 5°C (9°F) colder.
- **Recommended Attire**: Wear a windproof light coat or jacket. Bring a scarf and ensure hats have chin straps or clips when walking on suspension bridges.
